2. Core capabilities

Answers 24/7

Every call picked up in about two seconds, including nights, weekends and holidays. No voicemail black hole.

Qualifies before quoting

Checks the job against the contractor's own trade list and 25-mile service area before giving any number.

Prices from the real list

Furniture from $75, TV mounting from $90, smart locks from $80. No trip fee inside the service area. Every quote carries “confirmed once we see the job”.

Escalates emergencies in seconds

Gas, leaks, power loss and no-heat ring the contractor's mobile immediately, then retry at 5 and 15 minutes, then text him.

A real person on request

A second number reaches a human directly. The owner's own numbers are whitelisted and never routed through anything.

The machine does the typing

Human-handled calls are recorded, transcribed and summarised the same way, so the paperwork happens without the paperwork.

3. What it will never do

A receptionist that over-promises is worse than no receptionist. These are hard limits in the AI's instructions, not settings.

It will notInstead it
Promise a final pricegives a range and says it is confirmed on site
Quote licensed electrical or plumbing worktakes only minor jobs, refers everything else
Accept appliance, pest, or pool workdeclines and names the right kind of company
Install a central AC or upgrade a panelrefers to a certified specialist
Take a full kitchen or bath gutrecommends a general contractor
Claim a trade licencestates it is an insured Texas LLC, which is what it is
Say it is open on Sundayit is closed, and says so
Offer Venmotakes cash, check, Zelle, and card with a 3% fee
Why this is the feature. A machine that stays inside its limits is cheap and safe. The value is that it never needs to be supervised, and it never puts a price on a job the contractor cannot honour.
